## 2. Overview

This online demo of SpatialAIM shows precomputed results on public datasets. There is no
registration, no upload, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising. All content (including
fonts and scripts) is delivered from our own server; visiting the demo transfers no data to
third parties.

## 3. Hosting and access data

The demo runs on a server operated by [hosting provider / data centre, address]. When you
visit it, the server necessarily processes your IP address, date and time, and information on
your browser and operating system in order to deliver the page and secure the connection. These
data are not stored in access logs. [If they are logged: state the retention period, e.g. "Logs
are deleted after 7 days."]

Legal basis: [Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure operation) — for public bodies:
Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR in conjunction with Art. 4 BayDSG].

## 4. Session data

During your visit the server keeps your selections (dataset, view, cell-type names you set) in
memory. They are never stored permanently and are deleted when the session ends (when you close
the tab).

## 5. Cookies

The demo sets a single, technically necessary cookie (`_streamlit_xsrf`) that protects the
application against cross-site request forgery. Legal basis is § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG; no consent
is required.

## 6. External links

Links to other websites (e.g. GitHub, the sources of the datasets, GeneCards) are only opened
when you click them. The respective provider is responsible for any data processing there.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17),
restriction of processing (Art. 18) and to object (Art. 21). Please contact the controller
named above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority,
e.g. [the Bavarian State Commissioner for Data Protection (public bodies) or the Bavarian
State Office for Data Protection Supervision (private bodies)].
