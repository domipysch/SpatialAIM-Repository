## 2. Overview

This online demo of SpatialAIM showcases the tool using precomputed results on public datasets.
There is no registration, no upload, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising.

## 3. Processed data

When you visit the demo, the server necessarily processes your IP address, date and time, and information on
your browser and operating system in order to deliver the page and secure the connection.

## 4. External links

Links to other websites (e.g. GitHub, the sources of the datasets, GeneCards) are only opened
when you click them. The respective provider is responsible for any data processing there.
